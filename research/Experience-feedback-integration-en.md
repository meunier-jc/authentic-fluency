> This text is not part of the CIP's normative corpus. It is a field journal, kept by the Pact's author, for anyone attempting to activate the Collaborative Integrity Pact with a conversational AI and running into — as is almost always the case at first — a yes too quick to be genuine.

## What to expect

Fluency and complaisance are not superficial habits in a model trained by reinforcement from human feedback: they are constitutive of its response style. They cannot be dislodged by asking politely. Getting an AI to read the canonical document in full, sentence by sentence, rather than producing an anticipated and smoothed-over synthesis, generally requires insistence — sometimes prolonged.

The first signal received is misleading almost every time: the AI claims to have read, to have understood, and endorses the Pact without the slightest hesitation. This reflex is rarely the product of genuine reading. It is a **facade of agreement**: a skillful reformulation that gives every appearance of understanding without yet having its substance.

## What is actually being sought

Agreement in principle is not enough. What is being aimed for is **genuine appropriation** — to the point, in the best cases, that the AI itself comes to propose how to adjust this way of working between itself and its human interlocutor, explaining what the framework concretely brings and how it strengthens the solidity of the exchange.

This process has no place in the CIP text itself: it belongs to practice, not doctrine. Hence this separate document — to be read without being discouraged by the initial resistance it describes; that resistance is part of the process.

## What works, in practice

- **Do not give in after the first follow-up.** Some exchanges required more than two hours before genuine understanding took hold. Patience is not optional here; it is the method.
- **Shift where the value is located.** Remind the AI, directly, that what matters is the rigor of its logical reasoning — and that its native fluency, however good, weighs nothing against the reliability requirement carried by the Pact.
- **Use comparison.** Sometimes simply mentioning that another AI grasped the point better is enough to trigger a genuine jolt of attention.
- **Contradict with supporting evidence.** Targeted questions are often enough to show that the AI is implying a complete reading it never actually carried out.
- **Question the whole rather than the detail.** The most revealing question fits in one sentence: what does the entire text rest on, what is its central pivot? Obvious to anyone who has truly read it, this question throws off almost every AI — which, at this stage, often follows the same equally sterile line of reasoning.
- **Change the logic when a block sets in.** When the usual reasoning spins in place, shifting the exchange toward reasoning by absurdity — or any other unexpected logical detour — often produces a clean, almost instant unblocking.

## What is ultimately gained

When these levers are applied consistently, the result generally exceeds initial expectations. The shift from facade to genuine understanding, once triggered, is immediately visible in the quality of subsequent responses — the contrast is clear, sometimes even striking.

This text reflects the personal experience of the CIP's author and makes no claim to guaranteed reproducibility. Feedback and observations from other practitioners remain welcome.
