## Evidence policy

Public technical documentation distinguishes direct evidence, author declarations, interpretations and hypotheses. A wording correction does not turn a claim into independently verified evidence. Historical documents remain available in the `archives/` directory and are labelled as historical sources.

## Language policy

The public and technical layer is maintained in English. French source reports and audit records are kept separately under [`docs/fr/`](../fr/). Translations must identify their source and must never overwrite an original document.
