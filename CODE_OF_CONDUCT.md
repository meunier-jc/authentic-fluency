# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this open-source research project respectful, constructive and accessible to people with different backgrounds, expertise and viewpoints.

## Expected behaviour

Participants should communicate in good faith, critique ideas and evidence rather than people, distinguish observations from interpretations, cite sources where relevant, respect uncertainty, and welcome well-supported disagreement. Protect the privacy of contributors and research participants by removing personal data, credentials, confidential prompts and private logs from public material.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, impersonation, deliberate exposure of private information, fabricated evidence, plagiarism and repeated disruptive conduct are not acceptable. Disagreement with the framework or with another contribution is not, by itself, unacceptable; it should be expressed precisely and respectfully.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content that violates this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented where appropriate and applied without regard to a participant’s status or agreement with the project.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer through the contact details in the README rather than publishing sensitive information in an Issue or Discussion. Include the relevant URL, a factual description and any context needed to investigate. Do not include private data that is not necessary for the report.

This Code of Conduct is intended to support open technical and research debate. It should be read together with [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md).
